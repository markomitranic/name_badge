UC8276 4-gray calibration

Companion to NameBadge.GrayTune. The LUT knob is the white-push frame count N per gray level (0 = black, ~18 = white). Goal: find the two mid counts whose reflectance matches what the dither pipeline assumes for pixel values 85 and 170.

1 · Quick check: on-screen reference patches

Click a patch to fullscreen it (Esc to exit), hold the badge next to the screen. First turn screen brightness down/up until the white patch roughly matches the panel's white band under your room light — only then judge the mids. Disable True Tone / Night Shift. Emissive vs. reflective is never a perfect match; use this for sanity checks, the photo method below for numbers.

#000 · black
#555 · dark (85)
#AAA · light (170)
#FFF · white

2 · Measurement: photo analyzer

On the badge run GrayTune.probe(a, b) — it draws black(0) | a | b | white(18), so each photo carries its own black and white reference. Photograph straight-on in even, diffuse light (no flash, HDR off if possible), load the photo here, then click bands in this order:

  1. white band (right)
  2. black band (left)
  3. each probe band you want measured

Each click averages a small region, converts sRGB→linear, and reports reflectance normalized between your black and white clicks — lighting and exposure cancel out. Tune until the dark probe reads ≈9.1% and the light probe ≈45.7%.
